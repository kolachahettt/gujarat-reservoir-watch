What this number decides

“Ravi Season — Mahi Right Bank Canal (Shedhi Branch and Meshwo weir scheme) command area is based on the available live storage from Kadana Dam. Therefore, planning in Ravi season is based on the available live storage from Kadana Dam.”

Gujarat Narmada, Water Resources, Water Supply and Kalpsar Department — Data Bank, canal rotation schedule for the Mahi Right Bank Canal command (scheme page)

This page publishes that input. The rabi command area is set by how much live storage a dam has when the season is planned, so the figure above is not a status light — it is the quantity the decision is made on. The department's rotation schedule opens the first rabi canal run on 16 October.

What this page cannot tell you is how many hectares go unwatered. That conversion needs a water duty — depth applied per hectare per season — and no published source carries one per scheme. The honest ceiling is the department's own record of the most each command has ever actually irrigated: 131,988 of 243,867 hectares, or 54%, across the 31 schemes that report it. Even in its best year, about half of the commanded area does not get water.
